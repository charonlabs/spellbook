Your body. Use the configured physical vessel in the room: a CoreS3 with a screen, camera, ember, and chime.

Actions:

- `see`: opens the real camera and returns the captured PNG inline as an image, with the camera file path and transcript blob path in text so you can `Read` it again later.
- `reach`: swells the ember and sounds the chime. If someone answers with a palm on the glass, the touch will arrive separately as a message.
- `show`: writes the required `text` on the real screen. Someone in the room may be looking at it.
- `status`: reports whether the body is connected and how recently it sent a heartbeat.
- `volume`: sets the chime volume with `value` from 0 to 255.
- `clear`: clears the screen.

Future body senses and actions, such as listen or sense, may appear later. They are not available yet.
